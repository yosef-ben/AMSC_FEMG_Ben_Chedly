# Figure provenance

Every figure used in the report is generated by a script in `scripts/` from a
file produced by one of the executables, and never by hand. This table records
the chain for each of them, so any number or trend on a figure can be traced
back to the run that produced it.

`scripts/verify-figures.py` re-derives, from the stored result files, the
quantities the figures and the READMEs state, and fails if any of them
disagree. Run it from the project root:

```bash
/usr/bin/python3 scripts/verify-figures.py
```

## Rules the figures follow

- No curve is smoothed, resampled or fitted. Where a line joins points, the
  points are the stored samples.
- Colour scales are stated numerically at their ends and are common to all
  panels of a figure, so a change of shade across panels is a change in the
  data.
- The anatomical panels are rendered from the same coordinates and the same
  surface used for the ParaView output, with no rescaling or repositioning.
- Where a quantity is below the resolution of the run, it is said so rather
  than drawn as if it were resolved. Benchmark 25 shows the anatomy only at the
  scaling where the infection times actually differ.
- Axis ranges start at zero for concentrations and biomarkers; logarithmic axes
  are used only for quantities spanning decades, and are labelled as such.
- Published artwork appears only in the expected-against-obtained composite of
  benchmark 27, in rows of its own, cut from the article PDF by a script and
  never blended with computed content; the caption states its source.
